# Beacon Design System: Trust & Transparency

## Concept
An AI chatbot support platform focused on trust and transparency. We reject the generic "SaaS blue" and "AI purple-pink" in favor of a grounded, authoritative, and calm aesthetic that reinforces reliability.

## Color Palette
- **Backgrounds**: Warm off-whites (e.g., `#F9F9F8`) for the chat UI to reduce eye strain, deeper desaturated charcoal (e.g., `#1A1A1A`) for the admin interface to focus attention on data.
- **Primary Action**: A muted, earthy terracotta or sage green (e.g., `#5B7B7A`) instead of electric blue. 
- **Text**: Deep slate (e.g., `#2C3030`), avoiding pure `#000000`. Minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio strictly enforced.
- **Confidence/Escalation State**: A distinct, calm amber (e.g., `#D98324`) to signify transition to a human, avoiding alarming reds or generic grays.

## Typography
- **Primary Font**: *Outfit* or *Geist* (No system defaults like Inter/Arial).
- **Hierarchy**: Strong typographic contrast. Headings are slightly tightened, body copy is relaxed for readability.

## Components
- **Citations**: Inline, subtle, but distinctly interactive. Expandable to show exact source text.
- **Chat Bubbles**: Flat or very subtly bordered. No heavy drop shadows. No cards nested inside cards.
- **Escalation Module**: A distinct UI block, not just another chat bubble, to represent the human handoff.

## Anti-patterns
- No Inter/Arial/system-default font left untouched.
- No purple-to-blue or "AI purple-pink" gradient hero.
- No gray text on a colored background (keep 4.5:1 contrast minimum).
- No pure black/gray without a tint.
- No rounded-square icon tile floating above every heading.
